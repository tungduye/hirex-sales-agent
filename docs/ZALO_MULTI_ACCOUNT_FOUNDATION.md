# Zalo multi-account foundation

This phase changes no live Zalo session and sends no provider message. The existing QA account remains the only account eligible for the reviewed manual QA `SEND_TEXT` path. Newly configured HireX Zalo accounts receive `READ_RECEIPTS` only; campaigns, AI and automation do not gain Zalo send access.

Each account has its own `channel_accounts` row, stable `external_account_id`, encrypted bridge credential, operator switch and provider health. HireX resolves the account server-side from a signed inbound event or an existing conversation. The Inbox account filter and conversation label use safe display names, not provider IDs. An account must be connected, operator-enabled and have `HEALTHY` or `DEGRADED` provider health before inbound is persisted. Outbound still requires the exact configured QA account, `SEND_TEXT`, an eligible conversation and a fresh provider-health check.

In the isolated Deplao QA runtime, the original `HIREX_ZALO_QA_ACCOUNT_ID` and `HIREX_REAL_INBOUND_CUTOVER_AT` remain account A. Optional `HIREX_ZALO_ADDITIONAL_INBOUND_ACCOUNTS` is a JSON array of up to ten objects with exactly `accountId` and `cutoverAt` (strict UTC ISO timestamp), for example `[{"accountId":"synthetic-b","cutoverAt":"2026-09-17T12:00:00.000Z"}]`. It is an inbound-only allowlist, not a login or credential. Do not configure a real account B until it has been independently authenticated on the Deplao side and reviewed. Each cutover is independent; historical spool rows remain frozen. Delivery rotates across configured accounts while preserving each account's spool order. The adapter callback outbox schedules at most the oldest due event per account in a batch, preventing one account from starving another.

Add-account order: authenticate the account in Deplao through the operator-controlled provider flow; confirm a single normal ConnectionManager listener; configure its stable identity and encrypted bridge credential in HireX; check its own health; then explicitly add its inbound cutover policy. Do not enable `SEND_TEXT` for B in this phase. Disabling or disconnecting a HireX account blocks new routing without deleting historical conversations or logging out Deplao. No database migration or live account B onboarding is performed by this foundation change.
